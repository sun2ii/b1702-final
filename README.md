## What We Do

Most technology problems don't start with technology.

They start with unclear decisions, broken processes, disconnected systems, or simply not knowing what to fix first.

We start by understanding what's actually happening before deciding what should change.

### Diagnose — Starting at $5,000

Every engagement starts here.

We learn how your business actually works, where things are getting stuck, and what is worth solving.

Sometimes that means building something. Sometimes it means fixing what you already have. Sometimes the right answer is to leave something alone.

The goal is simple: know what you're solving before you spend money solving it.

### Build

Have something that needs to exist?

We take it from 0 → 1.

A website. An internal tool. An automation. An integration. A new product. Or an entire software platform.

### Care

Already have something that works?

We keep it working.

Updates, improvements, fixes, support, and someone who already understands what you built and why.

### Grow

Built something good?

Let's get it in front of more people.

Strategy, content, SEO, campaigns, and the systems behind turning attention into actual business.

## We Work Best Together When

- The decision-maker is part of the conversation.
- We can see how the business actually works.
- We're allowed to question assumptions.
- Evidence matters more than defending old decisions.
- We're solving the right problem, not just the most obvious one.
